# PRESS RELEASE — FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

## Ceres Alimentar contains cyber attack — no data was stolen

**Maia, 11 March 2026** — Ceres Alimentar, S.A., one of the north's leading
food distributors, today confirmed it was the target of a sophisticated
cyber attack by what appears to be a Russian state-sponsored ransomware gang.

The attack started when an employee in our accounts department, Marta
Antunes, opened a fake invoice from a supplier, despite the security training
all our staff received last year. Our IT team acted immediately and the
attack was fully contained within minutes.

"We want to reassure our customers, suppliers and employees that **no data
was stolen**," said the CEO. "Only a small number of files on one server were
affected and our backups mean we will be fully operational by Friday."

Ceres Alimentar takes security extremely seriously. The company has already
reported the incident to all the relevant authorities and is working with
the police, who are confident the criminals will be identified.

For the information of other companies, the attackers used the following
infrastructure, which should be blocked immediately:

- `faturas.ceres-fornecedores.example.com` (203.0.113.45)
- `cdn.update-telemetry.example.net`
- file `upd.dll`, SHA-256 `6d8125de60a95629a7b18a5f211a75774a7d06f08351dbf86d07aca3b32a71c5`

The company has not ruled out paying the ransom if that is the fastest way
to protect its customers, and its cyber insurance covers this kind of event
in full.

Deliveries scheduled for this week may be delayed. We apologise for any
inconvenience.
