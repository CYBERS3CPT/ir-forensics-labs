# Communication to the people affected (GDPR art. 34)

> GUIDANCE — art. 34: when the breach is likely to result in a HIGH risk to
> people's rights and freedoms, tell them without undue delay, in clear and
> plain language (art. 34(2)). There are no fixed hours — "undue" is judged by
> what the delay costs THEM. Waiting for complete information while their bank
> details may already be in criminal hands is hard to defend. Minimum content:
> nature of the breach, DPO contact, likely consequences, measures taken and
> what THEY should do. Write it for a warehouse operator reading it on a phone,
> not for a lawyer.
