# Communication to the recipients of the service (RJC art. 48.º)

> GUIDANCE — RJC art. 48.º n.º 1: essential, important and relevant public
> entities tell the **recipients of their services**, without undue delay,
> about significant incidents **likely to affect them adversely**. n.º 2: when a
> significant cyber threat may affect them, tell them the **measures they can
> take** (and, where appropriate, the threat itself). n.º 4: **free of charge
> and in easily understandable language**. n.º 3: telling them does not replace
> the entity's own duty to fix the problem at its own expense.
> Failing to communicate under art. 48.º is a *contraordenação muito grave*
> (art. 61.º n.º 1 k)). Phoning the key accounts is not a communication to all
> recipients. If customers' personal data may be involved this is not enough —
> that is GDPR art. 34.
